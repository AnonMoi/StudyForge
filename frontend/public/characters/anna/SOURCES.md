- 角色页：https://makeine-anime.com/character/
- `main.png`：官方角色立绘 `assets/img/character/img_main02.png`
- `face.png`：官方角色表情图 `assets/img/character/img_face02.png`
- `main-shadow.png`：官方立绘阴影层 `assets/img/character/img_main02_shadow.png`
- `thumb-on.png` / `thumb-off.png`：官方角色缩略图 `assets/img/character/thumb_chara02_on.png` / `thumb_chara02_off.png`
